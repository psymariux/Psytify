# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Psytify to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Keep discussions on topic and technical disagreements about the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberately unwelcoming conduct.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate.

To report a problem, use [private vulnerability reporting](SECURITY.md) or contact the maintainer through
their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
